SEVEROV / ACCESSIBILITY

ACCESSIBILITY

Target: WCAG 2.2 AA.

  • This is a strobe-adjacent design, so the reduced-motion setting is a real requirement here. With it on, the marquees stop, the wordmark does not split, the waveform ribbon is not drawn, the hero video does not play (a still image shows instead), the live pulse is off, and every section renders in its final state.
  • Nothing flashes more than three times a second anywhere, including in the hero loop.
  • The hero loop is silent and has a pause control that stays on screen.
  • The waveform ribbon is decoration. It does not react to audio, and it is hidden from screen readers.
  • No audio plays on its own. Players load only when you press play, with the platform's own visible controls.
  • Spoken content in recorded sets has a written transcript.
  • Ticket states are written in words, never colour alone.
  • The rider is readable HTML with numbered clauses. Every element in the stage plots is also listed in a table.
  • Monospace metadata is never smaller than 11px. Tap targets are at least 24px.
  • Keyboard focus is always visible, and there is a skip link on every page.

Something not working: management@severov.demo.
