Who we are

HALFLIGHT Recordings runs this site and the mailing list for SEVEROV. Postal address: Unit 4, Wapping Wharf Yard, Bristol BS1 6XX, UK (demo address). This address is in the footer of every email.

Mailing list

Lawful basis: consent, under UK GDPR and EU GDPR. Every box is unticked until you tick it. Double opt-in: one confirmation email, nothing else until you click it. We store the time, IP address, the exact wording you saw, which segments you chose and which form you used.

The promoters and industry segment holds business contacts. Those are still personal data under UK and EU law and are treated exactly the same way.

Emails are sent by our email service provider, [ESP NAME — confirmed at launch], acting as our processor. Unsubscribed addresses are kept only on a suppression list; everything else is deleted within 30 days. Inactive subscribers are removed after 24 months. We never import lists from promoters, venues or other artists.

US subscribers: every email has a working unsubscribe link honoured within 10 days, our postal address, and a subject line that matches the content.

Booking enquiries

Enquiry details go to the agent for your territory (Lowland Artists or Ninth Street Touring) and are kept for 3 years for contract and tax records, then deleted. The form uses a hidden honeypot field and a timing check instead of a CAPTCHA; no third-party anti-spam service sees your data.

Embedded players

SoundCloud, YouTube, Bandcamp, Spotify and Apple Music players are not loaded until you press play, so they set no cookies until then. After that, the platform's own privacy policy applies. YouTube is embedded in privacy-enhanced mode.

Ticket links

Ticket links go to each event's official ticketing partner or promoter. We do not receive your purchase data.

Your rights

Access, correction, deletion, or a copy of your data: privacy@halflight.demo. We reply within one month. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.
