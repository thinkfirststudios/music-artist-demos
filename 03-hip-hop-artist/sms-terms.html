What you get

Recurring automated marketing texts from CASS EMORY when a song drops, when a capsule opens early for the list, and occasionally a link to something unreleased.

How often

Message frequency varies, roughly 2 to 4 messages a month.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

Stopping and help

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe; you will get one confirmation text and nothing after it. Reply START to rejoin. Reply HELP for help, or email sms@cassemory.demo.

Not a condition of purchase

Consent to texts is never required to buy anything, and buying something never signs you up.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. Your number is not sold or shared for anyone else's marketing.
