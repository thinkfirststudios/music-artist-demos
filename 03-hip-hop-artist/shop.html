SHIPPING

Everything ships from Houston within 5 to 7 business days. US orders arrive 2 to 5 days after that. International orders ship too; duties are collected by the carrier. You get a tracking number by email from the store platform.

RETURNS

Wrong size? Exchange within 30 days if it is unworn. Damaged in transit? Send a photo to shop@cassemory.demo and it gets replaced. Numbered editions and cassettes cannot be exchanged unless they arrive damaged.
