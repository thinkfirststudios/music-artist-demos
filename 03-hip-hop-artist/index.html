CASS EMORY HAS BEENPUTTING OUTA SONG EVERY SIX WEEKSSINCE 2024.

The tapes were written on the drive between a night job on the east side and a house in Baytown, which is forty minutes with no traffic and considerably longer with it. That drive is on most of the records somewhere.

Parking Lot Prophet was the first one with a live band on it, cut in a warehouse over two weekends with four people who have played on everything since. The singles that followed kept the band and lost the ceremony.

Everything is independent. The masters, the videos, the shirts, the list. Nothing here is waiting on a quarter.
