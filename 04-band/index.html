WHO'S IN THE VAN

BAND PHOTO — PLACEHOLDERDez Ferraro

Dez Ferraro

Lead vocals, rhythm guitar

Writes the words, usually last, usually in the van. Spent six years as a night-shift dispatcher before the band could pay for itself, which is where about half the album's imagery comes from. Plays a semi-hollow through a cranked combo and refuses to use a pedalboard with more than four things on it.

BAND PHOTO — PLACEHOLDERRuthie Okonjo

Ruthie Okonjo

Lead guitar, backing vocals, arrangements

The band's actual musical director. Studied classical guitar for nine years, quit, and has been usefully annoyed about it ever since. Responsible for the two-guitar interlocking parts that are the band's whole sound and for the fact that nobody in Nine Mile Static plays a solo.

BAND PHOTO — PLACEHOLDERSal Brennerman

Sal Brennerman

Bass, keys, backing vocals

Joined in 2021 after the original bassist moved to Denver. Runs the band's books, the merch inventory and the spreadsheet that decides whether a routing works. Plays a short-scale bass with flatwounds and adds the synth pads that keep the record from being only guitars.

BAND PHOTO — PLACEHOLDERKit Halvorsen

Kit Halvorsen

Drums, percussion

Came up playing in three bands at once and can still be heard counting under their breath. Hits the snare harder than the room usually wants and has never once been asked to play quieter twice.
