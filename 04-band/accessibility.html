ACCESSIBILITY

This site targets WCAG 2.2 Level AA.

  • Body text is bone on pitch, well above 4.5:1. Flare orange on pitch doesn't meet 4.5:1 for body copy, so it's only used for large display type, chips, rules and icons, never paragraphs.
  • Every interactive element has a visible 2px focus ring. There's a skip link and one H1 per page.
  • The date filters and the gallery lightbox work by keyboard and announce changes. The lightbox keeps the photo credit under the image; Esc closes it and the arrow keys move between photos.
  • Every image has alt text. The decorative halftone and hatch layers are hidden from screen readers.
  • The stage plot has a written text equivalent and the input list is a real table.
  • Videos have captions on by default, and the live video with speech has a transcript.
  • No audio plays automatically, ever. Players only load when you press play, and use the service's own controls.
  • With reduced motion on, everything renders in its final state, the hero halftone and ticker stop, and nothing moves, scales or loops.

Something not working? management@ninemilestatic.demo

Nine Mile Static is a fictional band created for a portfolio demonstration. The members, album, songs, tour dates, venues, support acts, press quotes, publications, booking agency, management, prices and contact addresses on this site are all invented. No real band, label, venue, festival, publication or ticketing company is represented here.

Band photographs on this demo are labelled placeholder frames. Environmental and live photography is royalty-free stock (Unsplash / Pexels) used as placeholder and is not the property of, or a depiction of, any real band. In a live build every press photo carries its photographer's credit in the visible markup.

No audio is hosted on this site. In a live build every player is an official embed from the licensing service. This demonstration uses non-functional labelled placeholders or explicitly Creative Commons audio, credited where used.
