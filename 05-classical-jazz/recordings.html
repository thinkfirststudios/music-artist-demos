Bach — Suites Nos. 1, 3 and 5, BWV 1007, 1009 and 1011. Recorded over four nights in an empty hall, no audience, no edits longer than a phrase.

“I had access to a hall for four nights in January and nobody in it. The room is the second instrument on this record and I have not tried to make it behave.”

Odile Rannoch is a fictional cellist created for a portfolio demonstration. The biography, ensemble, concerts, halls, orchestra, conservatory, record imprint, commissioned composers and their works, publications, management, fees and contact addresses on this site are all invented. No real musician, hall, orchestra, festival, competition, conservatory, record label, publication or presenter is represented here.

Repertoire note: The standard repertoire listed is genuine public-domain music — works by Bach, Beethoven, Brahms, Schumann, Fauré, Debussy, Chopin, Schubert, Haydn, Boccherini, Saint-Saëns, Dvořák, Elgar, Franck, Janáček and Rachmaninoff are free for anyone to perform. Listing them here is realistic and carries no claim of any real performance. All contemporary works and their composers are invented. The compositions are public domain; recordings of them are not, and are licensed separately.

No competition results, awards, prizes, certifications, chart positions or streaming figures are claimed anywhere on this site, and none should be added to a live build without documentary evidence.
