Programme notes

I write my own. Presenters are welcome to reproduce these for their programme books — please credit and don't cut them in half.

Bach — Suite No. 5 in C minor, BWV 1011

The one where you retune the instrument

Scordatura drops the A string a whole tone, and the cello stops sounding like itself for twenty-five minutes. That is not a side effect. It is the piece.

6 min read · 30 August 2026Debussy — Sonata for Cello and Piano, L. 135

Eleven minutes, and not one of them settled

Debussy called it Pierrot angry with the moon, then withdrew the description. I have played it eleven ways and I still do not know which one is right.

5 min read · 12 July 2026Ilya Renko — Fenwold (2024), for solo cello and fixed media

Writing for a cellist and a tape machine

Ilya sent me forty seconds of a recording of an empty room and asked what I could do against it. This is a note about what I couldn't.

Reproduction

Presenters are welcome to reprint any of these notes in a programme book. Please credit ‘Note by Odile Rannoch’ and reproduce it whole. Email press@ if you need a different length and I'll write one.

Two or three letters a year

Not a newsletter. Two or three times a year I write a proper letter — where I'm playing next season, what I've been recording, and usually a few hundred words about a piece I've been arguing with. Studio places and masterclass dates are announced here first, and presale links for my own dates go out to this list before anywhere else.

Two or three a year. Unsubscribe from any of them in one click.
