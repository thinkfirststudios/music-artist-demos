Handwriting

I found your list in the pocket of a coat,eggs and a stamp and call about the roof,the y in Tuesday leaning like it did,like it was hurrying to you.

You can lose a voice, they tell me that,the sound goes first and then the face,but I could pick your capital Rout of any stack of mail in any state.

ChorusHandwriting, handwriting,the last thing that's still in your hand,I keep it in the kitchen drawerwith the matches and the rubber bands.

I haven't called about the roof.The eggs I bought. The stamp I usedon a letter I don't know where to send,so it's in the drawer with you.

A note from Bryn

My grandmother's shopping lists are still in a drawer in my mother's kitchen. Nobody has thrown them out and nobody has said why. This is the song that named the record, and it was the last one finished.
