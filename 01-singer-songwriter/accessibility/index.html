Accessibility

This site aims to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA.

  • Every page can be used with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a skip link to the main content.
  • Ticket states are written in words (“Tickets”, “On sale Fri 3 Oct”, “Sold out”), not shown by colour alone.
  • Nothing plays on its own. Players load only when you press play and use each platform's own visible controls.
  • Every video has written captions and a full transcript on the page.
  • Every lyric is published as real text, not as an image.
  • The stage plots in the press kit have a written description and input tables alongside the diagrams.
  • If you have asked your device to reduce motion, the site shows everything in its final state with no animation.
  • Every photograph has a text description, and its photographer's credit is written beneath it.

Access at shows

For wheelchair spaces, companion tickets, seating near the stage for hearing or sight, or anything else that would help, contact the venue's box office first. If they cannot help, write to management@brynhollis.demo and we will sort it out with them.

Tell us what is broken

If something on this site does not work for you, write to management@brynhollis.demo.
