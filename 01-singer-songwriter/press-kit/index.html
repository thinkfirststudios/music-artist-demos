Long bio · 400 words

Long bio

Bryn Hollis grew up between a farm road and a river town in eastern North Carolina, the kind of place where the bank becomes a church becomes a flea market and nobody holds a ceremony about it. She has lived in Durham since 2014. The songs she writes are mostly about work, weather, and the people who stay: a night-shift lamplighter, a road that only goes one place, a letter that never got sent.

Her debut, Tall Grass Country (2018), was recorded to tape in two days. The Salt Year (2021) was her first record with the trio, and Porch Sessions, Vol. I (2023) went the other way: four songs, one microphone, one afternoon. Across those records the writing has got plainer and the arrangements have got quieter, and she has come to treat restraint as the main instrument.

Handwriting, her fourth record, was written over two winters in a rented house outside Durham and recorded a mile from it, in January and February 2026. Hollis produced it with Wes Calloway, who also plays drums and percussion. Marta Cruz-Obi plays upright bass and Ronan Deeds plays pedal steel on six of the eleven songs. The windows were open for most of the sessions, which is why there is a car passing on track six, What the River Kept. It was the fourth take and it was the right one, so the car stayed. The record was mixed by Iona Serrell and mastered by Peter Vahn, and the sleeve design and hand-lettering are by Ines Batalha. Three songs were released ahead of the record: Lamplighter in February, Orchard Road in April and The Long Field in August.

Hollis is fully independent. She owns her masters, licenses the records through a small distributor, publishes through Long Field Songs (BMI) and makes most of the year's living from ticketed shows and the merch table at those shows, where she is usually standing. She plays around seventy nights a year in seated rooms of 200 to 600 people, solo in the North East and with the trio from Chicago west, and has played most of those rooms more than once. A meaningful part of any given audience has been coming since the first record.

Every lyric she has written is published in full on her site, and the answer to anyone who asks to record one is usually yes.

Stage plot and input list

Solo

Stage plot, written out

Audience is downstage. Bryn sits centre stage on an armless stool. Vocal mic (input 1) is directly in front of her; the guitar DI (input 2) sits at her feet, stage right of the stool. A small table is to her left, stage left of the stool, for water, capos and a tuner. An optional small guitar amp sits upstage of her right shoulder, stage right, with a mic (input 3). One wedge, mix 1, is downstage centre pointing at her. One power drop is needed at centre stage for the amp and tuner pedal.

Trio

Stage plot, written out

Audience is downstage. Bryn sits centre with vocal mic (input 1) and guitar DI (input 2), with her optional amp just upstage right of her (input 3). Marta Cruz-Obi stands stage right with the upright bass: bass DI from the pickup (input 4) and a bass mic (input 5). Ronan Deeds sits stage left at the pedal steel, with his amp upstage of him and a DI from the amp's line out (input 6). Wes Calloway plays a compact kit upstage centre: kick (7), snare (8), and two overheads (9 and 10). Three wedges on two mixes: mix 1 downstage centre for Bryn; mix 2 on two wedges, one in front of the bass and one in front of the steel, which the drummer also hears. Power drops at stage right for bass, at stage left for the steel amp, and at centre for guitar.
