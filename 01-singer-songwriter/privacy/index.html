Privacy

Plain language. Demo content: this page shows what a live site would say.

Who sends the letter

The mailing list is run by Long Field Songs on behalf of Bryn Hollis. Sender name: Bryn Hollis. Postal address: Long Field Songs, PO Box 3140, Durham, NC 27702, USA (demo address). That address appears in the footer of every email.

What we collect and why

Your email address, and if you give them, your first name and nearest city. The city is used only to tell you when a show is near you. When you sign up we record the time, your IP address, the exact consent wording you saw and which form you used, so we can prove you asked to hear from us.

Lawful basis and double opt-in

We rely on your consent. The box is never ticked for you. After you sign up you get one confirmation email and nothing else until you click it.

Processor and retention

Emails are sent through our email service provider, [ESP NAME — confirmed at launch], which acts as our processor and stores the list on our behalf. If you unsubscribe we keep only your address on a suppression list so you are never added again; everything else is deleted within 30 days. Inactive subscribers are removed after 24 months.

Ticket links

Tickets are sold by each venue's official ticketing partner. When you follow a ticket link you leave this site and that partner's privacy policy applies. We never receive your payment details.

Embedded players and video

Bandcamp, Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube players are not loaded until you press play. Until then no third-party player cookies are set. Once you press play, that platform's own privacy policy applies. Video uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode.

Store

The store is a hosted storefront. Orders, payment and addresses are handled there, not on this site. Buying something never adds you to the mailing list.

Access and deletion

To see what we hold about you, correct it or delete it, write to privacy@brynhollis.demo. We reply within 30 days.

Unsubscribe

Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom and it works in one click. We honour every request within 10 days, usually immediately. You can also do it here:

Demo form, submissions are not stored.
